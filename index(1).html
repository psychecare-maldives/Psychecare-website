ABOUT PSYCHECARE

Modern thinking. Compassion at the centre.

PSYCHECARE Mental Health Group is being developed in the Maldives to bring a range of mental health services together in a professional, respectful and supportive care environment.

OUR PURPOSE

Make mental health care more accessible.

We aim to support individuals and families through careful listening, appropriate assessment and collaborative care.

OUR COMMITMENT

Dignity, privacy and respect.

Care should be individualised, culturally thoughtful and guided by professional standards and confidentiality.

OUR SERVICES

Specialist support across the mental health journey.

Our planned services include assessment, diagnosis and therapeutic support for individuals, children, adolescents and families.

◉

Clinical Psychology

Psychological assessment and therapeutic support for emotional and behavioural wellbeing.

⌂

Child & Adolescent Psychology

Developmentally appropriate psychological support for children, adolescents and families.

✚

Adult Psychiatry

Psychiatric assessment, diagnosis and treatment planning by qualified medical professionals.

♡

Marriage & Family Counselling

Support for relationships, communication and family wellbeing.

⌕

Psychological Assessments

Structured assessment to better understand strengths, needs and areas of difficulty.

↗

Addiction Counselling

Non-judgemental support focused on recovery goals and coping strategies.

◎

Psychological Evaluation

Individualised evaluation to inform recommendations and care planning.

◇

Psychological Diagnosis

Clinical diagnostic assessment by qualified professionals within their scope of practice.

Services are planned areas of care. Availability and professional staffing will be confirmed before appointments are offered.

THE PSYCHECARE APPROACH

Technology-forward. Always people-first.

We believe modern systems should support—not replace— the human relationship at the heart of mental health care. Clinical decisions remain with qualified professionals.
